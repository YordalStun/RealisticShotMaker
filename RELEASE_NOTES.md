Cinematic documentary insert shots of old photos — instant prints or framed photos in real, path-traced rooms —
rendered with Blender Cycles up to 8K.

## Which download?

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| **RealisticShotMaker-Setup-….exe** | Installer. Adds Start menu shortcuts for the program and the Render Server. |
| **RealisticShotMaker-Portable-….exe** | Portable. Unpacks to a folder and runs from there (a USB stick works); keeps its settings and renders beside it. |
| **RealisticShotMaker-Portable-….zip** | The same portable program as a plain zip. |

Blender is included; nothing else to install.

**Needs:** Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). An NVIDIA RTX card is recommended: it runs the ray-traced lens model with
OptiX. Other graphics cards render with Blender's standard lens; the CPU works too, slowly.

## What's in it

- **Your photo, aged for real:** instant films (Polaroid 600, SX-70, Instax, Spectra) with fading, scratches,
  fingerprints, stains, creases, folded corners, curl and handwritten captions. Or a photographic print in a picture
  frame: seven frame styles, three print sizes, optional card mount, glass. Optionally reshape the print or frame
  to fit your picture.
- **Sets:** study desk, kitchen table, attic trunk, bedside table, mantelpiece, investigation board, photo line,
  shoebox of photos, photo album, and for framed photos a hallway wall of family photos and a sideboard of frames.
- **Camera:** documentary moves (push-in, slides, arcs, rack focus, tilt and crane reveals), multi-photo tours and
  montages, fun moves (whip pans with hidden cuts, crash and dolly zoom), and moves made for frames. A shot list
  with any number of cuts, each shot with its own move, framing, lens and aperture, already moving after each cut.
- **Real camera behaviour:** a ray-traced lens model (breathing, bokeh, fringing), film and digital camera bodies,
  operator sway, focus-puller drift, motion blur.
- **Light and look:** times of day, weather and dark lighting moods (noir blinds, bare bulb, torch, candle, TV
  glow, neon, police lights, lightning, moonlight, red room); a film lab for grain, halation, bloom, lens dirt and
  grades.
- **Working:** a quick draft preview (optionally rasterised for speed; final renders are always ray traced), a 3D
  view of the camera path, a render queue that resumes stopped renders and keeps the PC awake while it works, and
  live frames as they render.
- **Render Server:** export a shot as one `.rsmpack` file and let another PC render it from a watched folder, with
  its own queue, progress and preview.
